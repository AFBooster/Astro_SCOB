🃏 Object Cards

Printable one-per-object cards — a fact, where to look & the right scope. Place them beside each telescope. Science Centre Observatory, Jurong.
← Dashboard
Tip: print double-sided or on card stock, cut along the boxes, and laminate for reuse on the observing deck.
Facts are docent talking points — keep it conversational. Positions aren't shown here (they change nightly) — use the dashboard or Tonight's Tour for what's up. The interface is available in Singapore's four languages; object names and the facts stay in their catalogue/English form as docent guidance. · Science Centre Observatory, Singapore.
