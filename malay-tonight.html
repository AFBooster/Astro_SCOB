⛵ What the star-pictures mean 🐉 Chinese sky tonight 🪷 Indian sky tonight

Altitudes, directions, the Sun's buruj and Venus are computed on-device for SCOB (1.3342°N, 103.7357°E) at the chosen moment (Sun, Moon & planets via the shared astro-core.js engine), so the page works offline. Each star-picture is listed by its brightest marker star. The buruj the Sun stands in uses the sidereal zodiac (Lahiri ayanāṁśa). Bintang Timur (morning) / Bintang Kejora (evening) is the planet Venus. The Malay sky is a living oral tradition and regionally varied — see The Malay Sky for the star-pictures and sources.
