3 The three deck rules

  • No white light. Phones on dim, red mode if you have it — one white torch ruins everyone’s night vision for 20 minutes.
  • Don’t touch the tube. Guests look with their eyes, not their hands; a nudged scope loses the target and you re-align.
  • Set expectations, then delight. “It’ll be a small fuzzy glow — but that glow is 10 million stars / a planet 600 million km away.” Managed expectations make it magic.
