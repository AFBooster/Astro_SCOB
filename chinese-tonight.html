🐉 What the mansions mean二十八宿釋義 ← Night-sky dashboard 觀星主頁

Altitudes, directions and the Moon's lodge are computed on-device for SCOB (1.3342°N, 103.7357°E) at the chosen moment (Moon position via the shared astro-core.js engine), so the page works offline. Each mansion is shown by its marker (determinative) star. The 🌙 lodge is the Moon's real position among the stars; the 📅 almanac value is the separate calendar mansion (a fixed 28-day cycle for date-selection, matching the Japanese koyomi), which drifted from the sky long ago — the two rarely agree.
