🐉 What the mansions mean ← Night-sky dashboard

Altitudes and directions are computed on-device for SCOB (1.3342°N, 103.7357°E) at the chosen moment, from each mansion's standard determinative (reference) star — so the page works offline. Rising / setting is from the star's hour angle; "near the meridian" means it is close to its highest point due north/south. For what each mansion means, see The Chinese Sky.
